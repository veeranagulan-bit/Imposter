HOW TO PLAY

Imposter Ink Rules

1
Get your secret

Players see the secret word. The imposter gets only a clue.

2
Draw — don’t write

Show the idea with drawings. Never write letters, numbers, initials, or spell the word.

3
Chat without spoilers

Discuss the drawings, but do not type or directly reveal the secret word.

4
Imposter can guess

The imposter may guess the word during the round. A correct guess ends the match immediately.

5
Vote after drawing

Discuss the evidence and vote for the player you think is an imposter. You cannot vote for yourself.

6
Skip if unsure

If most players choose SKIP VOTE, the vote is skipped and the same word continues.

7
Two imposters

If there are two, catching one removes only that imposter. The remaining imposter stays in the match and a new word starts.

8
How to win

Players win by catching every imposter. Imposters win by surviving or correctly guessing the word.
