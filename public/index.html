Imposter Ink Rules

Know the rules before you create or join a room.

1
Get your secret

Players see the secret word. The imposter only gets a clue.

2
Draw — don’t write

Draw the word without writing letters, numbers, initials, or spelling it.

3
Use chat carefully

Talk about the drawings, but never reveal the secret word directly.

4
Imposter’s chance

The imposter can guess the word. A correct guess wins immediately.

5
Discuss & vote

After everyone draws, discuss who seems suspicious and vote.

6
Skip when unsure

If most players skip, voting ends and the same word continues.

7
Catch the imposters

With two imposters, catching one removes only that imposter. The match continues with a new word.

8
Win the match

Players win by catching all imposters. Imposters win by surviving or guessing the word.
